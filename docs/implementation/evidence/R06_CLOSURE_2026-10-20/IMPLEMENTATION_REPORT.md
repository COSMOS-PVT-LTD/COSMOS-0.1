## Outcome and scope

The previous R06.3/4 specified-component assessment is preserved. An **additive downstream solved candidate** now solves, instead of prescribing, core chamber pressure, oxidizer/fuel flows and mixture ratio. GG additionally solves shaft speed, GG pressure and both GG branch flows. Flow-dependent resistances and explicitly sourced bounded pump characteristics enter the residual system. Both pumps feed their actual core and GG withdrawals; no GG flow is omitted from pump demand. GG chemistry is recomputed at its actual O/F/pressure, with choked effective throat and turbine/shaft balance.

Two-way feedback is implemented: CEA HP chamber → non-adiabatic frozen nozzle → current gas-side recovery/Bartz/wall state → counterflow fuel heat/pressure → reactant enthalpy returned to CEA. Gas heat extraction is included in the nozzle energy equation; it is **not** the old adiabatic CEA RocketSolver with a hotter fuel temperature. Internal regenerative heat cancels once in a common-reference external energy ledger. Adiabatic pump work, pressure contribution to liquid enthalpy, frictional coolant heating, auxiliary load and shaft loss are accounted for consistently in the declared model. Independent dimensional conservation checks gate usability after nonlinear convergence.

The computational scope requested in this turn is implemented for **steady PRESSURE_FED and GAS_GENERATOR**, subject to the following deliberate model envelope:

- Single-phase constant-property incompressible liquids within explicit source-backed windows; regulated tank pressures, not pressurant sizing/blowdown.
- Infinite-area equilibrium chamber and adiabatic convergent; frozen calorically-perfect gas in a cooled divergent nozzle. This does not solve chamber/convergent wall cooling, variable-composition nozzle expansion, viscous CFD, shocks/separation or transients.
- Full-core-fuel counterflow regeneration. New solved-candidate mode refuses film mass injection and unsupported routing. The existing imposed-film/direct assessment is preserved separately, not silently promoted to a solved film model.
- User-supplied quadratic pump fits, efficiencies and validity windows, not universal or experimentally authenticated component maps. GG exhaust thrust is not credited.
- No staged-combustion/expander/electric cycles, phase-change conditioner, automatic hardware acceptance or automatic installation of candidate values into the original workflow.

These exclusions are visible in the API, result and Projects panel. A numerical candidate is not a production or physical acceptance record. **The two requested computational capabilities are implemented and locally qualified; the broader backlog and physical-validation gate must not be marked universally complete.**
