## 1. Ownership

COSMOS - Cryogenic Optimization and Simulation Multiphysics Operating System
("COSMOS") is proprietary software owned by **COSMOS PVT LTD**.

COSMOS PVT LTD retains all rights, title, and interest in original COSMOS
software and associated proprietary technical material, subject to applicable
third-party rights and licenses.

## 2. Proprietary Status

COSMOS is not open-source software. Public repository visibility does not grant
the public unrestricted rights to use, reproduce, modify, distribute,
sublicense, commercialize, or create derivative works.

The repository license is proprietary: **All Rights Reserved**. See `LICENSE`.

## 3. Copyright

Copyright © 2026 COSMOS PVT LTD. All Rights Reserved.

Copyright notices must be preserved in source files and governance documents
unless a documented exception is approved.

## 4. Third-Party Intellectual Property

Third-party libraries, frameworks, datasets, models, tools, and dependencies
remain subject to their respective licenses and ownership rights.

COSMOS PVT LTD:

- does not claim ownership of third-party software
- must preserve required notices and license terms
- must not replace third-party licenses with the COSMOS proprietary license

## 5. Contributor Responsibilities

Contributors must comply with applicable employment, contractor, confidentiality,
intellectual property assignment, and contributor agreements.

Contributors must not submit:

- third-party proprietary code without rights
- confidential information
- secrets or credentials
- material prohibited by `documentation/PUBLIC_REPOSITORY_IP_POLICY.md`

## 6. Confidential Information

Confidential company, customer, supplier, financial, and restricted technical
information must not be committed to the public repository without explicit
approval.

## 7. Public Repository Restrictions

The public repository is a controlled development surface. It is not a license to
publish all company information. See:

- `documentation/PUBLIC_REPOSITORY_IP_POLICY.md`
- `SECURITY.md`
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`

## 8. Future Licensing

Any future commercial, partner, government, or external licensing program for
COSMOS requires explicit authorization and legal review by COSMOS PVT LTD.

## 9. Patent-Sensitive Development

Engineering and software development may generate patent-sensitive information.
Contributors should avoid public disclosure of unpublished inventions and
patent-sensitive technical details without approval.

## 10. Software Provenance

COSMOS development should maintain traceability for:

- source authorship
- design decisions
- dependency introduction
- test evidence
- release approvals

## 11. Dependency Provenance

New dependencies must be recorded with:

- package name and version
- license
- purpose
- security considerations
- approval status

## 12. Architecture Governance

`documentation/COSMOS_0.1_FREEZED.md` is the authoritative COSMOS 0.1
architecture reference. Repository changes must not silently redesign frozen
architecture responsibilities without authorized architecture governance.

## 13. Legal Review

This document describes project IP governance practices. It is **not** legal
advice. Final legal documents, licenses, contributor agreements, and publication
decisions are subject to review by qualified legal counsel for COSMOS PVT LTD.

## 14. Company Ecosystem Positioning

COSMOS is the company's core engineering software platform. Future internal
systems may interoperate with COSMOS through controlled interfaces. COSMOS is not
required to contain every future company system within a single repository.
